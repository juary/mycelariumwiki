# Sources and editorial scope

This project is an independent English-language fan wiki for **Mycelarium by PLAN KAPKAN**, the Windows game published on Steam as app **5181520**. The separate free demo is app **5371480**. The project is not an official developer website.

The first content set adapts existing, source-based editorial research from the owner's [Mycelarium Wiki](https://mycelarium.top/). Its local source register is `docs/mycelarium-sources.md` in the separate `mycelarium-src` checkout. The register records official-source reviews through **November 20, 2026**, with some topic-specific reviews on November 16. Those research dates are inherited provenance; preparing this repository does not constitute a fresh gameplay test or a new check of every external page.

Article `updated` fields represent the dates of the source material's editorial revisions. They do not mean that this project independently played, measured, benchmarked or reproduced the mechanics. The 1.03 patch publication date remains **November 11, 2026 at 23:05 UTC**, separately from wiki revision dates. A patch being the latest announcement in a dated review is not a guarantee that no later patch will appear.

## Historical community evidence

The Light Shroom article briefly distinguishes pre-1.03 player reports from developer-confirmed changes. Its [historical Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/5181520/discussions/0/572679917415757945/) was reviewed in the original project on November 12. Reports about the red layer precede the generation rework and include conflicting results. They are not fixed coordinates, a current route, a confirmed bug or a promise about the separate demo.

## Content boundaries

- Advice such as a suggested learning sequence or upgrade comparison is identified as editorial guidance, not an optimal build established by testing.
- No invented recipes, unlock chains, production rates, refunds, search coordinates, control mappings, save-transfer rules or achievement counter rules are added.
- The mushroom reference lists supported names and roles; it does not claim to be a complete roster. Buildable mushroom names are separate from the five hidden magical-mushroom discoveries.
- The 1.02 Survival description is historical. The later 1.03 note specifically adds Great Migration and its achievement to Survival; it does not say every end goal and evolution task returned.
- Full-game patch notes and language support are not automatically attributed to the separate demo.
- Update summaries paraphrase developer announcements and do not present their optimization claims as benchmark results.
- The achievement overview avoids listing discovery and ending spoilers. Its official-source link leads to the complete Steam list, which contains spoilers.

## Artwork and rights

Game artwork and screenshots remain the property of their respective rights holders, including PLAN KAPKAN. An MIT license for this project's website code does not license the game or its artwork. Images inherited from the owner's earlier project are illustrative; their interface may predate the described patch and they do not prove a current location or an unlock route.

The earlier source register credits promotional forest art and screenshots to the official Steam asset endpoints, including [the promotional forest artwork](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/5181520/a7bdf06f3a9b4d0f97ae45aac90b1622790e00b3/library_hero_2x.jpg?t=1793730446). ## Maintenance

When the developer publishes a new patch, recheck affected mode, mushroom, resource, control and achievement claims against the original announcement. Recheck store requirements and languages against their current listings. Change an article's revision date only when its material has actually been reviewed, and preserve the distinction between patch publication, source review and repository publication dates.
